About us

Auravelle Cosmetics

Beauty in every you — a small cosmetics house in Salem, Tamil Nadu, building a short line of considered everyday skincare.

Our story

Auravelle Cosmetics was founded on the idea that good skincare does not need to be loud. Most people do not want a ten-step routine. They want two or three things that work, feel pleasant to use, and are honest about what is inside.

Kojiba is where we started: a brightening and nourishing bar built around kojic acid, niacinamide, jojoba oil and licorice extract. We chose a soap deliberately — it is the one step almost everyone already takes.

How we work

We design and market Kojiba from Salem and have it produced by a third-party manufacturer, which is standard practice for cosmetics of this kind. Every carton carries the manufacturer's details, the batch number and the licence number, so you can always see who made what you are using.
