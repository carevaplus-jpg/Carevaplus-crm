Questions about Kojiba

What is Kojiba?

Kojiba is Auravelle Cosmetics' brightening and nourishing soap — a 75 g bar formulated with kojic acid, niacinamide, jojoba oil and licorice extract.

How do I use it?

Wet skin, gently massage with Kojiba soap to create a rich lather. Rinse thoroughly with water. Use it in place of your usual cleansing bar, on the face or body.

Can I use it every day?

The carton states that Kojiba is suitable for daily use. As with any new cosmetic, patch test on a small area first and see how your skin responds before using it more widely.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

Kojiba is formulated to be gentle, and the carton states it is dermatologically tested. If your skin is reactive or you are treating a specific condition, check with a dermatologist before adding any new product.

What does kojic acid do?

Kojic acid is a naturally derived ingredient widely used in skincare for its effect on uneven tone. Results vary between people, and sun protection matters alongside any tone-focused routine.
