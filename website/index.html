Our care standards

Every Careva+ engagement is held to the same written commitments:

  • Consent first. No procedure without the patient's or family's informed approval.
  • Dignity always. Privacy, modesty and personal preference are non-negotiable.
  • Clinical boundaries. Our staff follow the treating doctor's plan and never prescribe.
  • Transparent billing. Rates agreed in writing before service begins.
  • Escalation path. A supervisor reachable within 15 minutes, any hour.
