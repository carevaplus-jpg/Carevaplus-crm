Careers

Join the Careva+ care team in Salem

We are regularly hiring nurses, care attendants and physiotherapists who want steady work, fair pay and proper supervision.

Open roles

Who we are looking for

Registered nurses (GNM / B.Sc.)

For home nursing shifts across Salem — injections, wound care, catheter and Ryle's tube care, vitals monitoring.

  • Valid qualification certificate
  • 12 or 24-hour shifts

Care attendants

Daily living support for elderly and bed-bound patients: bathing, feeding, mobility and companionship.

  • Experience preferred, training given
  • Male & female roles

Physiotherapists

Home visits for stroke, orthopaedic and geriatric rehabilitation, with session-wise or monthly schedules.

  • BPT qualified
  • Flexible visit hours

Japa maids & postnatal carers

Supporting new mothers and newborns with feeding, hygiene, massage and night care.

  • Experience with newborns
  • Live-in and day shifts

What we offer

  • Steady placements, not one-off gigs
  • Pay agreed in writing before a placement begins
  • A supervisor you can call during any shift
  • Training in home-setting protocols and emergency response
  • Work close to home, across Salem and nearby towns

How to apply

Call or send a WhatsApp message with your name, qualification and the area you live in. Our coordinator will arrange a short interview and a practical skills check.

Please keep these ready — it speeds up the process considerably.

  • Qualification certificate
  • Aadhaar / ID proof

  • Two references, if available
  • Details of previous experience
