About us

Care that treats the family as part of the plan

Careva+ is a Salem-based home health care service. We place trained nurses and caregivers in homes, supervise them properly, and keep families informed.

Who we are

Careva+ was built around a simple observation: most families in Salem do not struggle to find a caregiver — they struggle to know whether that caregiver is trained, supervised and doing what the doctor asked.

We operate from Alagapuram and serve Salem city and the surrounding towns. Our staff are recruited for clinical skill and for temperament, because both matter at a bedside. Every placement is backed by a nurse supervisor, written care standards and a named care manager the family can call.

Behind the scenes we run an AI-assisted care CRM, so enquiries are answered quickly, follow-ups are not forgotten and every shift is documented.

How it works

From your first call to ongoing care

A clear, supervised process — so you always know what happens next, who is coming and what it costs.

  1. 1

    Share the need

    Call +91 96555 08881, message us on WhatsApp or submit the enquiry form. A care advisor listens to the diagnosis, the daily routine and the family's situation — no obligation.

    Takes 10 minutes · Any hour

  2. 2

    Free assessment

    A nurse supervisor assesses the patient at home, or over a video call if you prefer. We check mobility, medication, wounds, feeding and risk of falls, then draft a written care plan.

    Free · Usually within 24 hours

  3. 3

    Written quotation

    You receive the care plan and the rate in writing before anything begins — shift length, duties, duration and cost. No hidden charges and no surprise add-ons.

    Agreed before service starts

  4. 4

    Matched caregiver

    We assign a professional matched to the condition, the language spoken at home and the shift you need. You are told who is coming, with their qualification and verification status.

    Most placements within 24–48 hours

  5. 5

    Ongoing supervision

    Scheduled supervisor visits, daily digital reports of vitals and activity, and a named care manager on call. If a caregiver is unwell or isn't the right fit, we replace them without restarting your billing cycle.

    For as long as care continues

Be prepared

What to keep ready for the assessment

None of this is mandatory — it simply makes the first visit faster and more accurate.

Discharge summary

If the patient has recently been in hospital, the discharge summary tells our nurse exactly what care was prescribed.

Current prescriptions

A list or photo of ongoing medicines, doses and timings, so medication support starts correctly from day one.

Daily routine

Waking, meals, bathing, physiotherapy and sleep times — we build the shift around the patient's existing rhythm.

Language preference

Tamil, English or another language at home. We match caregivers so the patient can communicate comfortably.

Home access

Stairs, lift, room layout and bathroom access, which decide equipment needs and fall-prevention steps.

Family contact

One person we should report to daily, plus an emergency contact if they are unreachable.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The things families ask us most often, answered plainly. If your question isn't here, just call — we would rather tell you honestly.

How quickly can a caregiver start?

For most attendant and nursing requests we can place a professional within 24 to 48 hours of the assessment. Specialised critical-care placements may take a little longer.

Are your caregivers background verified?

Yes. Every caregiver goes through identity and address verification, certificate validation, a reference check and a practical skills assessment before assignment.

Can we change the caregiver if it isn't working out?

Absolutely. Tell your care manager and we arrange a replacement — your billing cycle continues uninterrupted rather than restarting.

Do you provide medical equipment at home?

We arrange hospital beds, oxygen concentrators, suction machines, monitors, wheelchairs and ventilator support on rental, delivered, set up and tested by our team.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on the level of care, shift length and duration. You receive a written quotation after the free assessment — there are no hidden charges or surprise add-ons.

Do you coordinate with our existing doctor?

Yes. Our nurses follow the treating doctor's prescription and share daily reports so your doctor can adjust the plan with real data.

Which areas around Salem do you cover?

Salem city and the surrounding towns including Omalur, Attur, Mettur, Edappadi, Sankagiri, Valapady and Yercaud. If you are unsure, call us and we will tell you honestly whether we can staff your location.

Is the first assessment really free?

Yes. A nurse supervisor assesses the patient at home or over video and drafts a care plan at no cost, with no obligation to proceed.

Can you arrange care at short notice or at night?

We operate 24/7 and take calls at any hour. Night shifts and urgent placements are common — call the helpline and we will tell you what is realistically possible.

What happens in a medical emergency?

Call 108 immediately for an ambulance. Our caregivers are trained in first response and will stabilise and assist until help arrives, then inform the family and the supervisor.
