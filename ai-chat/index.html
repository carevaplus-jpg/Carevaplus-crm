Welcome to Careva AI

A free assistant that sends every message to the strongest open-source models on OpenRouter. If one model is busy it switches to the next, and Council mode combines several models' answers for hard questions.

Free account, no card needed. Free models allow about 50 messages a day, or about 1,000 a day after a one-time $10 credit top-up.
