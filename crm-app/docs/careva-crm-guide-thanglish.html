1App enna? Yaarukku?

Careva+ CRM — unga company-oda customers, leads, deals, tasks, quotes, invoices ellathaiyum oru edathula manage panna help pannura executive-level CRM app. HubSpot maadhiri periya CRM-la irukura main features ellam, unga phone-la simple-aa use panna mudiyum.

  • Yaarukku: Business owners, sales managers, sales executives, small & medium companies.
  • Enga data irukkum: Unga phone-kulla mattum. Account create panna vendaam, internet illama kooda (offline) full-aa work aagum.
  • Currency: Indian Rupee (₹) default — lakhs/crores format la (₹3.6Cr, ₹38.7L) kaatum. Deal-ku USD-um select pannalam.

2First time setup

App-a first time open panna "Welcome to Careva+ CRM" screen varum. Idhula 4 details kudukkanum:

FieldEnna podanum
Your name requiredUnga peyar — e.g. Karthik Raja
Your roleUnga designation — e.g. Managing Director
Your emailUnga email (optional) — emails log aagum bodhu idhu use aagum
Company name requiredUnga company peyar — e.g. Careva Plus

"Create my workspace" click pannina, app empty-aa, fresh-aa start aagum — demo data edhuvum irukkadhu. Oru ready-made sales pipeline (New Lead → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Won / Lost) automatic-aa create aagum. Neenga dhaan Admin.

Pudhu phone-aa? Munnaadi edutha backup file irundha, setup screen-laye "Restore from backup" click panni unga full data-vai thirumba kondu varalam.

3Dashboard — Executive view

App open panna first-aa varra screen. Unga business eppadi poyittu irukku-nu oru paarvai-la theriyum.

  • Get started checklist — pudhu user-ku: first contact, company, deal, products add panna shortcut. Ellam mudinja automatic-aa maraindhidum.
  • Open Pipeline — innum close aagaadha deals-oda total value.
  • Won This Month — indha maasam jeyicha deals value.
  • Win Rate — close aana deals la evlo % jeyichom.
  • Due Today — innikku mudikka vendiya tasks count.
  • Revenue chart — last 6 months won vs lost.
  • Pipeline by stage — ovvoru stage-la evlo value irukku.
  • Team leaderboard — yaaru adhigama deals jeyichaanga.
  • Recent deals & activity feed — latest updates.

4Contacts

Unga customers, leads, partners — ellarum inga. Contacts page-la:

  • New contact — first name, last name, email, phone, job title, company, status, source, owner, tags.
  • Search — peyar, email, company vechu udane theduralaam.
  • Status filter — Lead Prospect Customer Churned — customer journey-la yaaru enga irukaanga-nu paakalaam.
  • Sort — column header-a tap panni name/company/status vechu arrange pannalaam.
  • Contact page — avanga full details, Activity timeline (notes, calls, meetings, emails, WhatsApp, SMS ellam date-wise), avanga Deals, avanga Tasks.
  • Log activity — Note / Call / Meeting select panni enna pesuneenga-nu type panni "Log" pannunga. Team-ku history theriyum.
  • Edit / Delete — pencil icon menu-la.

5Email, WhatsApp, SMS, Call — one tap la

Contact page-la 4 buttons irukku:

ButtonEnna nadakkum

6Companies

  • Ovvoru client company-kum oru record — industry, size, phone, city, country, website, owner, notes, tags.
  • Company page-la: Open pipeline value, Won revenue, andha company-oda contacts list, deals, tasks, activity — ellam oru edathula.

7Deals pipeline — Kanban board

Ungaloda ellaa sales opportunities-um oru board-la columns-aa theriyum. Ovvoru column oru stage.

  • Drag & drop — deal card-a pidichu adutha stage-ku izhuthaa podhum. Column totals udane update aagum.
  • New deal — deal name, amount (₹ / $), contact, company, owner, expected close date, stage.
  • Person filter — "Everyone" illa oru particular team member-oda deals mattum paakalaam.
  • Deal page — stage stepper, Mark Won / Mark Lost buttons, activity timeline, tasks.
  • Overdue close date irundha red-aa highlight aagum.

Stages-a Settings-la rename / add / delete pannalaam.

8Tasks & Calendar

  • Task types: Call, Email, Meeting, Follow-up, To-do.
  • Priority: Low / Medium / High.
  • Due date & time, assignee (team member), contact / company / deal link, notes.
  • Tabs: My tasks · Overdue · Today · Upcoming · All open · Completed.
  • Checkbox tick panna task done — Completed tab-ku pogum.
  • Tablet / computer-la, left sidebar-la Tasks pakkathula due count badge theriyum.
  • Calendar — month view-la ella tasks-um date-wise. Oru date-a tap panni andha naal tasks paakalaam.

9Products, Quotes & Invoices

Products

Neenga vikkura products / services-oda price list — name, SKU, price, unit (per month, one-time…), description, active / inactive.

Quotes (Quotation)

  • Customer & deal select panni line items add pannunga — product, qty, price, discount %.
  • Tax % (e.g. 18% GST) — subtotal, tax, total automatic-aa calculate aagum.
  • Status: Draft → Sent → Accepted / Declined / Expired. Valid-until date.
  • Accepted quote-a oru click-la Invoice-aa convert pannalaam.

Invoices

  • Status: Draft · Sent · Paid · Overdue · Void. Due date, paid date track aagum.
  • Evlo payment varanum, evlo vandhudhu-nu clear-aa theriyum.

10Reports

  • Total won revenue, average deal size, overall win rate, companies count.
  • Won vs lost revenue — monthly bar chart.
  • Contacts by status — Lead, Prospect, Customer, Churned — ovvonnula evlo per irukaanga.
  • Rep performance table — ovvoru sales person-um evlo deals jeyichaanga, evlo value, win rate.

Monthly review meeting-ku idhu dhaan main screen.

11Search & Quick create

  • Mela irukura search bar tap panna — contacts, companies, deals ellathaiyum oru idathula theda command palette varum. Computer-la Ctrl + K.
  • Mela irukura blue "+" button — Contact, Company, Deal, Task, Quote edhuvanalum udane create pannalaam.
  • Left-la ☰ menu — ella pages-kum navigation.

12Settings & Team

  • Team — team members add pannunga (name, email, role). Deals, tasks, contacts-a avangalukku assign pannalaam; reports-la avanga performance theriyum.
  • Roles: Admin (ellam control) · Executive · Sales Rep. Admin mattum dhaan roles maatha / members remove panna mudiyum.
  • Sales pipeline — stages rename / pudhu stage add / delete. Won & Lost fixed.
  • Workspace & data — company name maathalaam, Backup / Restore / Erase.
  • Notifications & Outreach channels info.
  • Privacy policy link.

13Notifications — task reminders

Settings → Notifications → "Task due reminders" ON pannunga. Phone "Allow notifications?" kekkum — Allow kudunga.

  • Ungaloda task due time vandha, phone-la real notification varum — app close-aa irundhaalum.
  • Task-oda time maathina, reminder-um automatic-aa maarum. Task done / delete panna, reminder cancel aagum.
  • Phone restart aanaalum reminders thirumba set aagum.
Android 14+ phones-la exact time reminders-ku: Settings → Apps → Careva+ CRM → "Alarms & reminders" → Allow. Illana konjam late-aa varalaam.

14Backup, Restore & Privacy

Unga data unga phone-la mattum irukku — engalukku (developer-ku) edhuvum varaadhu. Adhanaala backup romba mukkiyam.

OptionEnna pannum
Back up nowFull data-vai oru .json file-aa create panni share menu open aagum — Google Drive, Gmail, WhatsApp edhula venaalum save pannunga.
Restore from backupSaved backup file-a select panna, full data thirumba vandhidum (current data replace aagum — confirm kekkum).
Erase all dataElla data-vum delete aagi, setup screen-ku thirumba pogum. Undo panna mudiyaadhu (Admin mattum).
Mukkiyam: Backup edukkaama app-a uninstall panna, data poidum. Vaaram oru thadava "Back up now" panni Google Drive-la vechukonga.
Privacy: No login, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. App edhuvum internet-ku anuppaadhu. Full policy: crm-app/PRIVACY.md (GitHub-la).

15App experience — "Liquid Glass" design

  • Apple Liquid Glass style — frosted glass panels, soft blur, gold highlights, navy executive theme.
  • Smooth animations — drawers slide aagum, cards lift aagum, buttons press feel.
  • Native Android app — edge-to-edge status bar, phone back button correct-aa velai seiyum, root screen-la back panna app exit aagum.
  • Haptics — save / delete / error-ku chinna vibration feedback.
  • Keyboard — type pannum bodhu form fields keyboard-ku keezha maraiyaadhu.
  • Fully offline — fonts kooda app-kulla bundled; internet thevai illa.

16Play Store la publish pannuradhu — step by step

Ungaluku anuppina files: careva-crm-1.0.0.aab (Play Store-ku upload panna), careva-crm-1.0.0.apk (direct-aa phone-la install panni test panna), upload key (keystore + password), store-listing images.

  1. Google Play Console account — play.google.com/console-la sign up pannunga. One-time fee $25 (~₹2,100). Company-aa register panna (Organization account) D-U-N-S number venum; individual-aa panna ID verification pannanum.
  2. Create app — App name: Careva+ CRM · Default language: English (India) · Type: App · Free · declarations tick pannunga.
  3. Testing first — Testing → Internal testing → Create release. careva-crm-1.0.0.aab upload pannunga. "Play App Signing" kekkum — Accept / Continue (Google unga app-a sign pannum; neenga vechirukkuradhu "upload key").
  4. Personal account-na: Google rule — production-ku munnaadi Closed testing-la kuraindhadhu 12 testers, 14 naal continuous-aa test pannanum. Unga team/friends Gmail IDs add panni, avangala opt-in link vazhiya install panna sollunga. Organization account-ku indha rule illa.
  5. Store listing (Grow → Store presence → Main store listing) — next section-la irukura text-a copy-paste pannunga. Images:
    • App icon: app-icon-512.png (512×512)
    • Feature graphic: feature-graphic-1024x500.png
    • Phone screenshots: screenshots/01…06.png (6 images, 1080×1920)
  6. Privacy policy URL — PR merge aana piragu indha link podunga:
    https://github.com/carevaplus-jpg/Carevaplus-crm/blob/main/crm-app/PRIVACY.md
  7. App content section-la ellaa forms-um fill pannunga (keezha table paarunga).
  8. Category: Business · Tags: CRM, Sales · Contact email: unga support email.
  9. Production → Create release → same .aab → Countries: India (illa ellaa countries) → Review & roll out. Google review-ku 2–7 naal aagalaam.

17Store listing text — copy-paste pannunga

App name (30 chars max)

Careva+ CRM

Short description (80 chars max)

Executive CRM: contacts, deal pipeline, quotes, invoices, WhatsApp & reminders

Full description

Careva+ CRM is an executive-grade CRM that puts your whole sales business in your pocket — contacts, companies, a drag-and-drop deal pipeline, tasks, quotes, invoices and reports, in a beautiful liquid-glass design.

★ EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD
See open pipeline, revenue won this month, win rate and today's tasks at a glance, with a 6-month revenue trend, pipeline by stage and a team leaderboard.

★ CONTACTS & COMPANIES
Keep every lead, prospect and customer in one place. Track status from Lead to Customer, see a full activity timeline, and link contacts to their companies.

★ ONE-TAP WHATSAPP, SMS, EMAIL & CALL
Message any contact on WhatsApp, by SMS or email — a pre-filled draft opens in your own apps — or call them in one tap. Every message is logged on the contact's timeline.

★ DEAL PIPELINE
A visual Kanban board for every opportunity. Drag deals between stages, filter by salesperson, mark deals Won or Lost, and customise your stages.

★ TASKS, CALENDAR & REMINDERS
Calls, meetings, follow-ups and to-dos with priorities and due times, a month calendar, and phone notifications when a task is due — even when the app is closed.

★ PRODUCTS, QUOTES & INVOICES
Build a product catalogue, create professional quotes with discounts and GST/tax, convert accepted quotes to invoices, and track what's paid and overdue.

★ REPORTS
Won vs lost revenue, contacts by status and sales rep performance for your monthly reviews.

★ TEAM & ROLES
Add your team with Admin, Executive and Sales Rep roles, and assign contacts, deals and tasks.

★ PRIVATE & OFFLINE
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone and works fully offline. Back up to Google Drive, email or WhatsApp in one tap, and restore on a new phone.

Built for founders, sales leaders and growing teams.
