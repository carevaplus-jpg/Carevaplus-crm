# Careva+ CRM — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 November 2026

Careva+ CRM ("the app") is a customer-relationship-management app for businesses. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

## The short version

- Everything you enter is stored **only on your own device**.
- The app has **no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking**.
- We **do not collect, receive, sell or share** any of your data.

## Information you enter

To work as a CRM, the app stores the information you type into it, for example:

- your name, role and email, your company name, and the names, emails and roles of team members you add;
- contacts and companies (names, phone numbers, email addresses, job titles, cities, notes);
- deals, tasks, activities, notes, products, quotes and invoices;
- a log of the emails, WhatsApp messages and text messages you start from the app.

This information stays inside the app's private storage on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party by the app.

## Messages and calls

The **Email**, **WhatsApp**, **Text / SMS** and **Call** buttons open the apps already on your phone (your mail app, WhatsApp, your messaging app or dialer) with a pre-filled draft. The app does not send any message itself. Whatever you send is handled by those apps under their own privacy policies. The app only saves a note on your device that a message was started.

## Backups

When you tap **Back up now**, the app creates a backup file and opens the Android share sheet. You choose where the file goes (for example Google Drive, email or WhatsApp). That destination's own privacy policy applies to the file. **Restore from backup** reads only the file you pick.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To remind you when a task is due (only if you turn reminders on). |
| Alarms & reminders / run at startup | To deliver task reminders on time, including after the phone restarts. |
| Vibration | For light haptic feedback when you tap. |
| Internet | To open links such as WhatsApp chats or a company's website. The app itself sends no data. |

## Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time from **Settings → Erase all data**, or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.
